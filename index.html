Keyboard shortcuts and voice dictation tips

⚡ Keyboard Shortcuts

Space (when not in editor) / Alt + M Toggle Microphone Start / Stop
Ctrl + S Quick Export / Download as Text (.txt)
Ctrl + Shift + C Copy Entire Transcript to Clipboard
Ctrl + F Toggle Find & Replace Bar

🗣️ Voice Punctuation Commands

When "Smart Punctuation" is enabled, say these words while dictating:

Say "period" or "full stop" ➔ .
Say "comma" ➔ ,
Say "question mark" ➔ ?
Say "exclamation mark" ➔ !
Say "new line" or "enter" ➔ ↵
Say "colon" ➔ :
